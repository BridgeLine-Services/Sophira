## Data we collect
- **Account information:** email, authentication identifiers, role, membership status.
- **Academic documents:** uploaded PDF/DOCX/PPTX/XLSX/CSV files and extracted text, handwriting-review images.
- **Assignments:** titles, instructions, requirements, statuses.
- **Teacher profiles and requirements:** course/teacher preferences you create.
- **Writing samples and feedback:** text you submit for feedback.
- **Learning patterns:** approved behavioral patterns used to personalize assistance.
- **Typing-speed information:** calibration test results and your selected baseline/pacing preference.
- **Research data:** research topics, retrieved source records, verification metadata, citations.
- **Activity/aggregate analytics:** owner-visible aggregates only (counts and statuses, not private content).

## Processing and AI
Content you submit is processed to provide the service, including by
**third-party AI providers** used for generation, verification, and feedback.

it is, by design, within the processing described above. External research
requests also cause third-party search/retrieval providers to receive your
research topics and, during verification, the URLs being checked.

## Local/offline processing (actual architecture)
When you enable the optional local model, generation happens ON YOUR DEVICE
and the content you submit for that request is not sent to a third-party AI
provider. Offline mode uses that on-device model and previously cached data.
The installed web app (PWA) also caches application files locally on your
device; that cache contains app code, not other users' data.

## Access controls and RLS
Row-level security isolates every user's private content. The owner can see
**aggregate** analytics only and cannot read your essays, assignments,
teacher notes, writing samples, learning patterns, research, uploads,
feedback, or typing data. These boundaries are enforced at the database
level, not merely in the interface.
